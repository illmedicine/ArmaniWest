Privacy

Draft for review by a lawyer before launch.

What we collect, and only with your OK

If you click "Sounds good" on the banner, we record: a random per-visit ID (to show how many people are online), the page you are on, whether you are on mobile or desktop, the site you came from, and your approximate city, region and country. To find that, your browser asks a third-party lookup service (ipwho.is), which sees your IP address. We do not store your IP address. If you click "No thanks", none of this runs.

Members

Signing in with Google gives us your name, email and photo through Google. We keep your points and settings. You choose whether you appear on the public leaderboard (with a display name you can edit) and on the fan map (city level only). You can switch both off any time on the Fanboys page.

Cameo requests

The name, email and message you submit are visible only to Armani and are used only to reply.

Who can see what

Visitor and member location details are visible only to Armani, through her private portal.

Your rights

Ask Armani to delete your member data or requests and it will be removed. California and EU/UK residents have additional rights; contact her through the site.
